      Travel essentials

      Rules change. Treat this as a starting point and confirm with official sources before you go.

      Permits

      Indian visitors who are not residents of Manipur need an Inner Line Permit (ILP), issued online or at Imphal airport on arrival. Foreign nationals should check the current Protected Area Permit rules with the Ministry of Home Affairs or their embassy.

      Manipur ILP portal

      Getting there

      Bir Tikendrajit International Airport in Imphal has direct flights from Delhi, Kolkata, Guwahati and Bengaluru. By road, NH-2 comes in via Kohima and NH-37 via Silchar; the nearest railhead is Jiribam.

      Getting around

      Auto-rickshaws and shared taxis cover Imphal and the valley. For the hills, hire a car with a local driver. Allow extra time: hill roads are narrow and weather can close them.

      Money and signal

      Carry cash outside Imphal; cards and UPI work in the city. Mobile data can be patchy in the hills and is sometimes restricted, so download maps offline.

      Where to stay

      Imphal has hotels for every budget around Paona Bazar and the airport road. Around Loktak and in Ukhrul, family homestays are the best way to stay, with meals cooked by your hosts.

      Travel wisely

      Conditions in parts of the state can change quickly. Check your government’s latest advisory and local news, travel with a registered guide in the hills, and carry ID and your permit at all times.

      When to go

      Every season has a reason. Most visitors come between October and April, when skies are clear and days are cool.

      October to November

      Clear skies after the rains, the harvest, and the Sangai Festival from 21 November.

      December to February

      Crisp, sunny days and cold nights near freezing. Migratory birds on Loktak.

      March to May

      Yaoshang, Lai Haraoba and, in May, the Shirui lily in bloom above Ukhrul.

      June to September

      The monsoon: the valley at its greenest, flowers in Dzükou, slower roads.
